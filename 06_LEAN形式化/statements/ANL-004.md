# ANL-004

For all integers `x`, the scaled Fenchel–Young identity holds at `p = -1`:
`x(-1) + (-1)x ≤ x^2 + 1`.

Unlike the conjugate of `exp`, the quadratic identity does not require `p > 0`.

- Objects: `Int`
- Quantifiers: forall x
- Assumptions: none; `p` is the negative unit
- Conclusion: inequality
- Boundary: `p < 0` remains finite
