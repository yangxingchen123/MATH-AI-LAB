# DISC-003

For all natural numbers `n`, the list of odd elements of `{1,...,n}` is sum-free:
no two (not necessarily distinct) members have their sum also in the list.

- Objects: `List Nat`
- Quantifiers: forall n
- Assumptions: `odds` is the recursive odd-number list in this module
- Conclusion: sum-free
- Note: reproduces a known construction; not a novelty claim
